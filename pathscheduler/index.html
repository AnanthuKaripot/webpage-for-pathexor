Complete NEET PG & INICET Preparation Platform

PG PathScheduler is your all-in-one study companion for medical entrance exams. Powered by AI and built by doctors for doctors, this app combines a massive question bank with intelligent learning tools to help you ace NEET PG, INICET, and FMGE.

Massive Question Bank

  • 22,000+ High-Yield MCQs covering all subjects
  • Subject-wise practice across 20+ medical specialties
  • Topic-wise quizzes for focused learning
  • QBank Archives featuring Past Year Questions (PYQs) and exam patterns
  • Regular content updates
  • Detailed explanations for every question

Pathexor AI - Your Personal Tutor

  • Get instant AI-powered explanations
  • Ask follow-up questions in natural language
  • Clarify doubts with conversational AI
  • Deep-dive into complex medical concepts
  • Search QBank with intelligent keyword expansion

Smart Study Planner

  • Create custom timetables with drag-and-drop
  • Track daily study streaks with heatmap
  • Set subject-wise goals and deadlines
  • Monitor time spent on each subject
  • Stay motivated with progress visualization

Adaptive Flashcards

  • Scientifically proven Spaced Repetition System
  • Auto-adjusts difficulty based on your performance
  • Create subject-wise flashcard decks
  • Convert notes to flashcards instantly
  • Track retention and mastery levels

Grand Tests & Mock Exams

  • Full-length NEET PG pattern tests
  • Custom test creator - choose subjects, topics & difficulty
  • Timed mode with realistic exam simulation
  • Detailed performance analysis
  • Compare scores with previous attempts

Advanced Analytics

  • Subject-wise accuracy breakdown
  • Weak topic identification
  • Guessing index detection
  • Fatigue pattern analysis
  • Time management & pacing insights
  • Rank predictor based on performance
